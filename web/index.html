What the model looks at

Each 16 px cell of the screenshot becomes a 9-bin histogram of gradient directions. Blocks of 2×2 cells are contrast-normalized, which gives 34,992 numbers. Two hundred shallow trees vote on those numbers, and a softmax turns the votes into the four probabilities above. The dark plate shows the histograms as lines.

99.28% test macro F1 on the X fake-profile dataset, trained on 11,000 screenshots. The dataset is synthetic and balanced, so this is not a moderation verdict. JPEG, WebP, or resizing can change the result; the benchmark uses lossless screenshots.
